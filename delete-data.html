Chase Data Deletion

How to delete data associated with the Chase app

← Chase Support & Legal

Delete your Chase data

Chase does not require you to create an account. The Chase records you create, including titles, amounts, names, categories, dates, reminders and notes, are stored locally on your device.

Delete individual records

You can delete an individual Chase from within the app by opening the Chase and choosing the delete option.

Delete all locally stored Chase data

To delete locally stored Chase app data, open Chase, go to Settings and use the Delete Data option. Follow the confirmation shown in the app.

Request data deletion

If you would like to make a privacy or data-deletion request concerning information associated with Chase outside your device, contact us using the email address below.

Data that may be retained

Chase Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by the app store and may use RevenueCat for subscription management. Transaction, purchase and subscription records may be retained by Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat or other applicable service providers in accordance with their legal obligations, security requirements and retention policies.

For more information, read the Chase Privacy Policy.
