Chase Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the Chase app

← Chase Support

Effective date: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chase ("Chase", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the Chase mobile application.

1. About Chase

Chase is an app for keeping track of refunds, repayments, deposits, reimbursements, credits, repairs, outstanding actions and other things you are waiting for.

2. Information you enter into Chase

You may enter information such as a title, amount, person or organisation, category, expected date, reminder and notes when creating a Chase.

This Chase information is stored locally on your device. Chase does not require you to create a Chase account and does not operate a server that receives or stores the Chase records you create in the app.

3. Notifications

Chase can use notifications to remind you about items you are waiting for. If you enable reminders, notification scheduling information is handled on your device using your device's notification services.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

4. Purchases and Chase Pro

Chase may offer optional paid features through Chase Pro. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the app store through which you obtained Chase, such as Google Play.

Chase may use RevenueCat to help manage subscription status and determine whether Chase Pro features are available to you. These services may process technical purchase information, transaction identifiers, subscription status and related device or app information needed to provide and restore purchases.

Chase does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Payment processing is handled by the relevant app store.

5. Bank accounts and email accounts

Chase does not connect to your bank account, payment account or email account. You enter the information you want to track manually.

6. Advertising

Chase does not contain third-party advertising.

7. Analytics and tracking

Chase does not use third-party advertising trackers. Third-party services used to provide app-store purchases or subscriptions may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

8. Data sharing

Chase does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only where necessary to provide features such as app-store purchases and subscription management.

9. Data retention and deletion

Chase records are stored locally on your device for as long as you keep them. You can delete individual Chase records within the app. Chase also provides an option to delete locally stored app data.

Removing the app may also remove locally stored Chase data, subject to your device's operating system, backup and restore behaviour.

Purchase and transaction records processed by Google Play, Apple or other service providers may be retained by those providers according to their own legal obligations and privacy policies.

10. Children's privacy

Chase is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We design Chase to minimise the information transmitted away from your device. However, no device, software or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Chase's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when appropriate.

13. Contact

If you have a privacy question or request concerning Chase, please use the contact information provided on the Chase Support page.
